      Controls

      Mouse: left click or drag uses the tool, right or middle drag pans, wheel zooms.

      Touch: tap to place, drag with the Pan tool to move, pinch to zoom, two fingers always pan, long-press to inspect.

      How to grow

      1. Draw roads. 2. Zone lots touching roads. 3. Power them with a plant in range. 4. Add a water tower for taller buildings. 5. Balance homes and jobs — watch the RCI bars. 6. Parks and schools raise land value.
